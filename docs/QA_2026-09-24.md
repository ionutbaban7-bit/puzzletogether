# PuzzleTogether + CoachingHub: release QA, 24 September 2026

Ten complex scenarios, combining actual user paths, integration checks and pixel comparisons. The source logs were produced for this revision in `qa-results/` outside the repository. A browser visual check of the deployed revision follows publishing and is recorded there.

| # | Scenario and expected outcome | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A first-time visitor sees PuzzleTogether puzzle identity, green/cream palette, and a direct image-selection route; CoachingHub keeps its own identity and defaults to forest green. Explicit light preference still works. | Puzzle UI smoke; CoachingHub 1,254 academy assertions, 54 mobile checks; post-deploy browser verification. |
| 2 | A shared `/create?activity=puzzle` link opens image/category selection. Mona Lisa and 100 pieces are chosen, reviewed before entering a name, and preserved on the way back to edit. | React UI smoke with catalog API, selection and back navigation. |
| 3 | Host creates a room with an access code, second player joins, guest is denied changing the puzzle, host changes to 192 pieces in the lobby and both receive the choice while the timer remains stopped; mystery option follows the saved choice. | `scripts/prestart-choice-test.mjs`. |
| 4 | Mona Lisa, Great Wave, The Kiss and Monet's Water Lilies are rendered as clipped pieces at five difficulties and both delivered and legacy board geometries; each of 4,200 sampled piece centers matches the full reference, including bottom/right edges. Portraits retain ratio in both reference sizes. | `scripts/image-scaling-test.mjs`. |
| 5 | Every active puzzle image and thumbnail is served and every licensed puzzle can create a room at every advertised difficulty, including portraits; delisted images cannot enter a new room. | 268/268 catalog serving assertions; 38 active image puzzles × 5 difficulties and 43 catalog entries with zero structural or provenance warnings. |
| 6 | Two participants can claim, move, lock, reset, sort and reconnect without duplicating pieces, inheriting each other's claims or losing room membership. | Protocol suite: jigsaw layout, claim lifecycle, puzzle reset and room snapshots. |
| 7 | A spectator cannot edit, a host can control stages, and chat is length-limited, ordered after reconnect and persisted after restart. | Protocol suite, 12/12 chat assertions. |
| 8 | Non-jigsaw rooms (canvas, sentence and coaching/emotion work) preserve team assignments, private answers, safety controls, progress and access restrictions after the visual changes. | Protocol suite; 13/13 workshop integrity assertions; UI smoke. |
| 9 | A collaboratively edited date survives a concurrent room echo and rejects a conflicting teammate edit; valid dates export with correct ICS day boundaries, escaped text and line folding. | React date UI test and calendar integrity assertions. |
| 10 | Public catalog images point to actual subjects and documented license pages; newly introduced image derivatives are non-empty, checksummed and never served during conversion. | Manual 38-image contact sheet, audited Commons metadata, 43/43 source records and 268 HTTP/catalog checks; atomic image conversion. |

The ten scenarios represent test coverage and a critical UX review, **not interviews or fabricated quotes from users**. Further prioritization should use feedback from the owner's real play session, especially touch controls and the explanatory text.
